Solid arrows carry the story forward. Dotted arrows loop back or sideways to a scene the child could already reach — 26 of the 60 transitions do, which is what lets a child wander and still finish. In the game there is no difference: a card is a card. The distinction is only here, to keep the forward path readable.

The eight cards that appear as arrow labels — each one is a real printed card, coloured the same way in the app:

The map, act by act

Six views, one per act. Dashed grey boxes are scenes belonging to another act, marked with that act’s number, so you can always see exactly which scene an arrow leaves and which one it reaches. Notice how many arrows converge on fox_meeting in act 4 — seven routes lead there, which is why that scene carries the reveal that Fina hid the crystal in the tower.

The four item gates

Items are never collected by scanning an item card — a scene grants them when the child enters it. These four scenes refuse entry without the right item, and no gate can dead-end the story.

Three ways it can end

All three are reached from crystal_room, by the last card the child places. None of them is a failure screen.

  • Golden✨ Magic

    Returns the crystal AND helps Fina's sister. Both problems solved.

    Returns the crystal, but Fina is left waiting.

  • Soft🏃 Run

    Goes home before deciding. No blame, just a nudge to try again.
